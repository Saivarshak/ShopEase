Image Management

Uploaded product, category, banner, and background images are stored persistently and reused across the storefront.

Supported Formats

JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WEBP uploads are accepted.

Persistence

New uploads are saved under media/uploads and referenced from the database.

Cleanup

Replaced uploaded files are deleted automatically when no record uses them.
